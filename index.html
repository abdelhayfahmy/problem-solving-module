Problem Solving

Beyond Symptoms – The Root Cause Approach

Welcome to this micro-learning module.

  • Not just putting out daily fires.
  • Systematic identification of root causes.
  • Eliminating problems permanently.

The Business Reality

Patient discharge delays and beyond

Patient delays are just one example of daily business challenges.

  • Stop treating surface symptoms (like just calming the patient).
  • Target the Root Cause to formulate a lasting solution.

The Solution: Fishbone Diagram

(Ishikawa Diagram)

A powerful visual framework to brainstorm and categorize potential causes systematically.

Maps out the entire process until the true root cause is exposed, eliminating guesswork.

The 4 Core Branches

Breaking down the patient delay problem:

  • People: Is the new staff fully trained?
  • Process: Are the discharge approval steps too complicated?
  • Equipment: Is the billing software crashing again?
  • Environment: Is chaotic noise slowing down communication?

Knowledge Check: Categorize

Scenario: High rate of dropped calls in the appointment center.









The 5 Whys Technique

Drilling down to the Root Cause

Ask "Why?" repeatedly (typically 5 times) to peel back the layers.

Example: Why did the server crash? ➔ Why wasn't it updated? ➔ (Root Cause Discovered)

Knowledge Check: Drill Down

Task: Sequence these events from Surface Problem (1) to Root Cause (5).

Quick Recap

Before the Final Assessment

  • Focus: Treat the Root Cause, not just symptoms.
  • Fishbone (Ishikawa): Map the problem across 4 core areas (People, Process, Equipment, Environment).
  • The 5 Whys: Drill down layer by layer until you find the ultimate failure point.

Final Assessment

1. Effective problem solving is primarily about treating symptoms quickly.
2. What is another name for the Fishbone Diagram?
3. Which is NOT one of the 4 core branches?
4. A chaotic, noisy ward falls under which branch?
5. Primary purpose of the 5 Whys?
6. When do you stop asking "Why"?
